# Excercises

Passing the course require:

1. Active participation in lectures, excercises/workshops and the final examination seminar
2. Completion of a mini-project
3. Presenting your project in the examination seminar

Navigate to the instructions for the different exercise/workshops in the menu to the left.

The pages for [your mini-project](../project/project-description.md) and the [examination seminar](../project/project-examination.md) give detailed information about what is expected of you in the course for receivng a passing grade.
