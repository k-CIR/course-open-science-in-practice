## Introduction
This data was collected at around the University campus in an effort to investigate staff and students energy-levels and general well-being. Questionnaire responses were collected anonymously on a digital platform either via provided tablet or the respondents own device (via QR code link).

The data presentation in lecture 2 describe the data and show some potential analysis that can be performed using the data. You find those slides [here](placeholder).

## Description
The dataset contain demographic variables, questions on habits surrounding sleep and screen time and 2 standard questionnaires:

- [WHO-5 Well being:](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01) 5 items measuring general well-being on a scale six graded (0-5) scale that can be summed for a total score. Higher score indicate more well-being.
- [PSQ-4:](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19996233/) 4 items measuring level of depression and anxiety type symptoms on a 0-3 scale, summed for a maximum total score of 12.
