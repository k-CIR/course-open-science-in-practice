Passing the course require, in part, the completion of a mini-project. During the course you should work on your project, writing a well-documented and commented analysis pipeline. Importantly, what will be evaluated is not the results of the project itself, but its reproducibility - how it is organized, documented and made available to others.

---

### Your project should be submitted as a public Github repository by email (link) to course organizer before the examination seminar.

---

An example of a well-structured project repository can be found here: [https://github.com/NiklasEdvall/an-approved-repo.](https://github.com/NiklasEdvall/an-approved-repo) Note, that this is an example and you are free to structure your project repository as you see fit. The most important thing is that it is well-documented and reproducible.

Simulated data for your project is available in 4 different simulated datasets, available for download under [resources](../setup/index.md). The datasets are presented in the lecture [mini-project and data overview](../lectures/lecture-data-overview.md). You are allowed (and ecouraged!) to simulate your own data.
